# Ploy

> Ploy is an AI-powered web marketing automation platform for growth teams. It helps teams build, optimize, and publish marketing sites, then use analytics, visitor identification, answer-engine optimization, and workflows to turn site activity into growth.

## What Ploy does

Ploy helps growth teams run their marketing website as an ongoing growth channel: build and ship pages, measure traffic, identify visiting companies, improve visibility in AI answers, and run repeatable marketing workflows.
